About Me

PhD candidate in Medical & Health Science, researching Clostridioides difficile within dairy systems under a One Health framework, with a focus on epidemiology. My research explores environmental reservoirs and potential transmission pathways, with particular focus on the role of dairy calves within the broader farm ecosystem.
